## 3. Detailed evaluation of the five opportunities

I would score them against six factors\:

- Market demand.
- Competition.
- Speed to launch.

### Website 1: Digital education and resource downloads

(Best for an early first sale)

**What I would build**

Rather than a generic education platform, I would create a collection of specialized learning products.

Potential product categories\:

|--------|--------|--------|
| Driving theory revision | Driving-test candidates | Paid practice access |

| Excel finance templates | SMEs and finance professionals | One-time sales |
| Interview preparation | Job seekers | Paid guides |
| Business templates | Entrepreneurs | Digital downloads |
| Professional exam preparation | Certification candidates | Subscription |

The driving-test category already has several competitors, so I would not prioritize it without a very strong differentiation.

I find accounting, finance and practical business resources more interesting as a starting niche.

**Why?**

You can produce a useful digital product, publish it, sell it repeatedly and automate delivery.

For example\:

- Payroll templates.
- Bank reconciliation templates.
- Cash-flow trackers.
- Budget templates.
- Basic accounting revision packs.
- Business expense trackers.

These can serve both Rwandan and international customers, with separate Rwanda-specific and general versions.

**Revenue example**

| Sales | Price | Revenue |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 40 template purchases | 2,500 | 100,000 |
| 30 revision packs | 5,000 | 150,000 |
| 20 premium bundles | 10,000 | 200,000 |
| **Total** |  | **450,000 RWF** |

This is an illustrative sales scenario, not a forecast.

**Competition and risk**

The biggest challenge is not building the website. It's creating materials that are accurate, useful and sufficiently different from free resources.

Do not simply upload copyrighted REB examination papers or paid third-party materials without permission. Create original explanations, quizzes and study materials, and verify the rights to anything you distribute.

**Technology\:** WordPress + WooCommerce or a lightweight digital-product system.

**Initial development estimate\:** RWF 50,000–120,000 if you do the design and development yourself, excluding your time and any paid content production.

**My verdict\:** Build this early. It has relatively low operational complexity and can generate revenue without requiring a large marketplace.

Property already has active competitors. Baza and Tura, for example, provide verified property discovery features.

**Initial development estimate\:** RWF 70,000–150,000.

**Main risk\:** Businesses will not keep paying if the website doesn't bring enquiries. You must demonstrate results.

**My verdict\:** Very interesting. I would launch one vertical first and reuse the same directory engine for future websites.

### Website 3: Jobs and tender aggregation

(High demand, higher maintenance)

This one interests me, but I would make a distinction between a general jobs website and a specialized opportunities intelligence platform.

The general job market already has established platforms\:

- [KORA](https://kora.mifotra.gov.rw/) – Rwanda's government job-search platform.
- [MIFOTRA recruitment](https://www.recruitment.mifotra.gov.rw/)\.
- [Job in Rwanda](https://www.jobinrwanda.com/)\.

For tenders, Rwanda already has the [Umucyo e-procurement system](https://umucyo.gov.rw/) and an [open contracting data portal](https://weeducate.rw/)\.  [Home](https://kora.mifotra.gov.rw/seeker/main?utm_source=chatgpt.com) [Rwanda Recruitment System Portal](https://www.recruitment.mifotra.gov.rw/us/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) [umucyo.gov.rw](https://umucyo.gov.rw/um/lg/moveLogin.do?utm_source=chatgpt.com) [OCDS](https://weeducate.rw/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

I wouldn't create another website simply reposting vacancies.

**What I would build instead**

A specialized opportunity aggregator with categories such as\:

- NGO and development-sector jobs.
- Finance and accounting jobs.
- Internships and graduate programmes.
- Scholarships and fellowships.
- International opportunities open to Rwandans.
- Public and private tenders.
- Procurement notices and supplier opportunities.

The value is organization and discovery.

A user should be able to filter by qualification, deadline, location, industry and opportunity type.

**Revenue model**

1. Employers pay to publish featured vacancies.
2. Organizations sponsor opportunity categories.
3. Premium users receive carefully curated alerts.
4. Employers pay for recruitment promotion.
5. Advertising supplements the income.

I would make basic job discovery free. Charging job seekers simply to access publicly available vacancies is a difficult trust proposition.

**Revenue example**

| Revenue | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| 10 featured employer posts × 15,000 | 150,000 |
| 100 premium alert subscribers × 1,000 | 100,000 |
| 2 sponsored opportunities × 50,000 | 100,000 |
| **Total** | **350,000 RWF** |

**Technology\:** Custom lightweight aggregator or WordPress with automated feeds and moderation.

**Initial development estimate\:** RWF 80,000–180,000.

**Main risk\:** Expired vacancies, duplicate posts, inaccurate information and the continuous need for fresh opportunities.

Do not scrape websites without checking their terms and permissions. Use official feeds, permitted public data, direct submissions and source links. Every opportunity should show its original source and deadline.

**My verdict\:** Worth building, but only with a focused niche and strong automation. It isn't as passive as it first appears.

**What I would build**

A specialized Rwanda travel guide with\:

- Destination guides.
- Detailed itineraries.
- Travel budgets.
- Accommodation recommendations.
- Local experience discovery.
- Tour operator directories.
- Practical travel information.
- Rwanda travel planning resources.

Possible content\:

**Revenue model**

- Affiliate links to accommodation and activity providers.
- Paid tour operator listings.
- Sponsored destination content.
- Direct booking referrals.
- Display advertising.
- Downloadable travel itineraries.

Important: affiliate commissions are not automatically 5–15%. Every partner has its own commission structure, eligibility conditions, attribution rules and payout requirements.

**Revenue example**

**Initial development estimate\:** RWF 60,000–130,000.

**Main risk\:** SEO takes time. International affiliate traffic may take months to develop.

**My verdict\:** Build it as a long-term traffic asset. It is not the quickest route to your first income, but it has attractive international reach.

**Model A: Free online tools**

Visitors use calculators or generators for free. You monetize through advertising, related products and premium features.

Examples\:

- VAT calculator.
- Loan calculator.
- Gross profit calculator.
- Depreciation calculator.
- Currency converter.

**Model B: Paid utilities**

Examples\:

- Quotation generator.
- Rent receipt generator.
- Business budget planner.
- Payroll report generator.

There is already competition in accounting software. For example, Qonta advertises plans from RWF 9,500 per month, including invoicing, inventory, payroll and tax features. [Qonta website](https://qonta.rw/)\.  [Accounting software for businesses in Rwanda](https://qonta.rw/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**A particularly interesting starting point: Rwanda Finance Tools.**

One website with\:

- PAYE calculator.
- VAT calculator.
- Net salary calculator.
- Payroll deduction calculator.
- Depreciation calculator.

**Main risk\:** You must maintain accurate calculations, tax rules and security. A calculator that produces incorrect tax figures can destroy trust.

**My verdict\:** Very promising if you build small, useful tools rather than trying to develop a full SaaS platform immediately.

## 7. How I would build them: speed first, economics second

I wouldn't use the same technology for all five.

| Website | Recommended technology | Reason |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Education resources | WordPress | Content, digital products and SEO |
| Business directory | WordPress initially | Fast listing and subscription functionality |
| Jobs/tenders | Custom or WordPress hybrid | Feeds, filtering and scheduled updates |
| Tourism | WordPress | Editorial content and SEO |
| Micro-SaaS | Custom code | Interactive tools and application logic |

### A few development principles

**Don't use Framer for everything.**

Framer is excellent for landing pages, editorial presentations and marketing websites. But when you need dynamic listings, user accounts, paid content, filtering, recurring billing and data management, WordPress or custom development will usually be more suitable.

**Don't custom-code what already exists.**

If a proven WordPress plugin can provide 80% of the functionality, use it. Spend development time on the features that differentiate your business.

**Use AI coding where it creates leverage.**

For example\:

- Generate calculator logic and test cases.
- Develop reusable listing components.
- Automate data imports.
- Build admin dashboards.
- Create enquiry-routing workflows.
- Generate structured SEO content drafts for human review.

**Keep each website technically independent.**

A problem with one website shouldn't bring the other four down.

# 11. My proposed next step: research before development

I think we should approach this as a small digital venture portfolio, not five random website projects.

I would start with a comprehensive market research and feasibility study covering all five websites individually, then compare them as a portfolio.

The output should be a practical investment document that tells us\:

- Which markets have identifiable paying customers.
- Which competitors are strong and where their weaknesses are.
- Which websites can generate revenue fastest.
- Which websites have the greatest long-term potential.
- Which technologies will get each website launched fastest.
- What to build, what not to build and what to postpone.
- How to reach RWF 1 million monthly combined revenue.
- How to manage all five without creating five full-time jobs for yourself.

My preferred strategy is therefore\:

**Five independent digital assets. One shared operating system. Five separate revenue experiments. One combined financial objective.**

That gives you the freedom to discover which businesses have the strongest economics rather than committing your entire budget to an idea before the market has spoken.
