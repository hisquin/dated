# 6. Three additional features I strongly recommend

These could make the platform much more valuable than a regular tourism directory.

**1\. Then &amp; Now**

Show how a location looked historically compared with how it looks today.

Particularly useful for historical buildings, settlements, royal sites and landmarks.

---

**2\. Understand the landscape**

Explain why a place has its particular geographical features.

For example, why the Virunga Mountains are volcanic, how lakes were formed, or how Rwanda's landscape influences settlement patterns.

---

**3\. Build your own Rwanda exploration trail**

Let users save interesting places and create a personal exploration list or itinerary.

For example, a historical exploration route through Nyanza, Huye and Butare.

## 7. One product decision I would make early

Don't force every discovery to be a tourist attraction.

A place can be worth discovering even if it has no ticket office, hotel, restaurant or established tourism infrastructure.

For example, a geographical landmark or a place with an interesting historical name might be valuable purely because of what it teaches people.

I would distinguish between\:

- **Discover\:** Interesting places and stories.
- **Explore\:** Places with geographical, historical or cultural depth.
- **Visit\:** Places with verified visitor access and practical information.

This distinction will help the platform grow into a genuine Rwanda knowledge and exploration resource rather than becoming another list of attractions.

**My biggest recommendation\:** Make the place detail page the heart of the product. If someone discovers a random location and spends five minutes learning its history, geography and significance, you've achieved something much more valuable than simply helping them find another tourist destination.
