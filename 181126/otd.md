# SCHOOL

_My proposed problem statement:_
> Early-career designers and developers can access plenty of learning resources, but many struggle to turn their knowledge into the ability to work effectively on a real digital product with a team, professional standards and commercial constraints.

__The difference between a course and an apprenticeship__

| Ordinary course model          | Arkon's proposed model                      |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Student purchases information. | Student develops professional capability.   |
| Watches lessons.               | Researches an actual market.                |
| Completes exercises.           | Works within a product team.                |
| Copies tutorials.              | Makes design or engineering decisions.      |
| Submits assignments.           | Builds and tests a working product.         |
| Receives a certificate.        | Presents the outcome and defends decisions. |
|| Receives code/design reviews.  |
